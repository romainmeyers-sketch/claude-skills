# Deck presets: the deck-type selector

Pick the deck type at intake (§0.2). Each preset sets the two dials (§0.3),
the typical structure, the slide devices to favor, and, critically, **where
the supporting detail lives** (on the slide vs. in speaker notes). Presets are
grounded in real Metis decks on the shared Drive; the exemplar links let you
open the canonical reference for each type.

> All presets keep the brand frame and palette fixed and obey the §0.7
> pre-flight. Presets change *density, structure, and where detail lives*,
> not the brand.

## Selector (quick map)

| # | Deck type | Dials D/V | Detail lives | One-line use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Readout / leave-behind** | 8 / 6 | On the slide | Final deliverable sent and read without a presenter |
| 2 | **Workshop / training** | 7 / 5 | On the slide + facilitation notes | Run a working session; pre-read + live activities |
| 3 | **Presentation** (balanced) | 5 / 5 | Slide + speaker notes | Projected live working session |
| 4 | **BD / proposal** | 5 / 6 | On the slide (persuasive) | Win the work: pitch, capabilities, proposal |
| 5 | **Current-state assessment** | 8 / 6 | On the slide | Evidence-heavy findings, maturity, gaps |
| 6 | **Exec / boardroom** | 2 / 3 | Speaker notes | One decision per slide for a senior audience |

---

## 1. Readout / leave-behind  (D 8 / V 6)

**Use when** the deck is the deliverable and will be read on its own. **Detail
on the slide**: it has to stand without narration.

**Structure:** cover, executive summary, section dividers, current state,
findings, recommendations, roadmap, operating model, appendix (assumptions,
methodology).

**Favor:** full-sentence titles that carry the so-what; dense tables and
heat maps; labeled diagrams and swimlane roadmaps; a `Source:` line on data
slides; an appendix for assumptions. Density swings slide to slide (a dense
table next to a single diagram).

**Exemplars:** LiveRamp AI-First Strategy Final Deliverable
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1s6A2FfWRX_IlQ48cYd0RUeGiKi2eDUFH/view`),
ROK AI Strategy Final Deliverable
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1UHQYd7plU3VXIPo-6OVRzpeIKrFoNOOF/view`),
Workday DEX Final Deliverable
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1gqg5cQ979JoAXOxf0SJj9N5S4U-bAT-n/view`).

## 2. Workshop / training  (D 7 / V 5)

**Use when** running a working session or teaching. The deck is a **pre-read
and a live facilitation tool**; detail on the slide, facilitation script in
speaker notes.

**Structure:** agenda, prior-session recap, **activity flow with numbered
STEPS and timeboxes** ("Breakout room: 30 min", "Close: 15 min"),
prioritization frameworks (a 2x2 plus a question bank table), large reference
matrices (opportunity x lifecycle stage), participant / breakout-room
assignment tables, close + next steps, appendix.

**Favor:** explicit step instructions; timings; board/Miro references; 2x2
quadrants with labeled corners; crawl/walk/run journeys; very dense reference
tables (these are working artifacts, not hero slides).

**Exemplars:** People + AI Strategy Sprint Workshop 2 pre-read
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1GfoiDliEfhnyqd33UJ3G07wEmMchtaNs/view`),
Genesys Workforce-planning Workshop masterdeck
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/10AVEsGHXBSX534sLnHXnkEx1GXvOMDee/view`),
NotebookLM Training
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1FM79hi8AYhEdbMOsyCyGMqkap4lmG9g1/view`).

## 3. Presentation (balanced)  (D 5 / V 5)

**Use when** projecting live in a working meeting. Key points + one diagram or
table per slide; **detail split between slide and speaker notes** (slide carries
the headline + visual, notes carry the elaboration).

**Structure:** flexible: title, agenda, section content, summary/next steps.

**Favor:** one dominant element per slide plus supporting detail; moderate text;
vary slide types so it does not feel like one template.

**Exemplar:** IT Product Management Introduction
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/15ldZYOimhC5FXxqZGaPDAbTvXXHmaYmk/view`).

## 4. BD / proposal  (D 5 / V 6)

**Use when** winning the work. Persuasive and polished, more breathing room than
a readout but still substantive; **detail on the slide** but in a selling voice.

**Structure:** cover, our understanding of your challenge, our approach /
methodology, illustrative workplan and timeline, our team (headshot + bio),
relevant case studies / credentials, investment, next steps.

**Favor:** case-study slides, team Speaker-Image layouts, capability maps,
phased-approach visuals, client logos. Asymmetric and confident.

> For a premier, photography-led client proposal as a **PDF**, use the
> `metis-premier-proposal` skill instead; use this preset for a PPTX pitch.

**Exemplars:** Alaska Airlines AI Adoption Proposal
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1K603snk1_B3Y6P489x1OG6Oyc5lEbO6W/view`),
loanDepot Collaboration Approach & Proposal
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1MWwCpskb6UPtIPoPJytEbxFjLrmCXZlo/view`),
Genesys Business Capabilities
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Ke3nBeLoxbuHIkw9KqL88GE1djuUrKiI/view`).

## 5. Current-state assessment  (D 8 / V 6)

**Use when** presenting findings and maturity. Evidence-heavy; **detail on the
slide.**

**Structure:** maturity framework + scoring (heat maps), dimension-by-dimension
findings, gaps vs. target, benchmark, interview/data synthesis, current-state
diagrams.

**Favor:** maturity heat-map tables with current-vs-target markers (color
encodes the gap, not decoration); scorecards; a "what it means" column;
`Source:` lines that cite interview counts and dates.

**Exemplars:** Global Foundries Current State Assessment & Recommendations
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Wm0LTH4-s1Tmima0buRpxe-BiZHFUQ2J/view`),
LiveRamp Current State Deliverable
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1sIvKbqMumHE9GZs0-IDE27ooX6IWmRA_/view`),
Workday Current State Assessment
(`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1TS-7MKXXMk2xBtDF__SeKyeIwYf4yRlj/view`).

## 6. Exec / boardroom  (D 2 / V 3)

**Use when** the audience is senior and the presenter talks. **One decision or
claim per slide, big type, minimal**: the depth lives in **speaker notes.**

**Structure:** a short arc: situation, the decision/ask, one supporting
visual, implications. Few slides.

**Favor:** a single strong statement; one chart; an explicit ask. Resist the
urge to fill the slide; push detail to notes and the appendix.

**Exemplars:** the executive-summary pages of the readouts above; IT Executive
Update (`https://drive.google.com/file/d/1AOW3DbToEj2GWA4z9I3YFIEpxbMXB5d9/view`).

---

## Notes

- Exemplars 1 (readout), 2 (workshop), and 5 (assessment) were profiled directly
  from the decks; 4 (BD) and 6 (exec) are anchored to the named exemplars and
  Metis BD/exec convention. To tune a preset to a specific deck, open its
  exemplar and match its structure.
- A deck can mix modes (e.g., a readout with an exec-summary opener at D 2). The
  preset sets the *default*; individual slides can deviate when the content
  calls for it.
