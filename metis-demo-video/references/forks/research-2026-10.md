Method source worth reading if scripting needs a doctrine: YC Startup School, "This Is How You Should Actually Make
Launch Videos" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v12aSIZIXUs): treat every demo as a launch, script it so it sounds
unscripted, pick the channel deliberately.

## Patterns across all twelve

- Logo timing depends on brand familiarity: unknown brands reveal at 7 to 10 s after the problem; known brands open or close on it.
- 7 of 12 AI products type the prompt on screen, then a "thinking" beat, then the reveal.
- Every claim gets one proof shot.
- Light/dark swaps mark chapters, or one colour flip marks problem -> solution.
- Every ending: logo + one line, then URL or CTA pill held 2 to 4 s. None ends on UI.
- Two cutting registers: agency explainer (new composition every 1 to 2 s) vs product-native (5 to 10 s UI holds).
